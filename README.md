# ☕ Retail Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales and customer behavior dashboard built using Power BI.
The dashboard analyzes retail coffee shop data to uncover insights into sales trends, product performance, and customer preferences.

It enables data-driven decision-making by identifying patterns across time, geography, and customer segments.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance over time
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify top-performing products and customers
* Evaluate sales distribution across countries
* Analyze the impact of roast type, size, and loyalty programs

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI – Data visualization & dashboard creation
* Excel/CSV – Data storage and preprocessing
* Data Analysis – Trend analysis, segmentation, KPI tracking

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

* Monthly and yearly sales trends
* Comparison across coffee types: Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, Robusta

### 🌍 Sales by Country

* Sales comparison across United States, Ireland, and United Kingdom
* Identifies top revenue-generating regions

### 🏆 Top 5 Customers

* Highlights highest-value customers
* Useful for targeted marketing and retention

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

* Order Date (Year & Month)
* Roast Type (Dark, Light, Medium)
* Product Size (0.2 kg, 0.5 kg, 1.0 kg, 2.5 kg)
* Loyalty Card (Yes/No)

---

## 📌 Key Insights

* Certain coffee types show consistent high sales performance
* United States contributes the highest revenue
* A small group of customers drives a large portion of total sales
* Loyalty programs influence customer purchase behavior
* Seasonal trends impact sales
